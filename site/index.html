CVD Prevention Gap Atlas

Where cardiovascular risk burden outpaces prevention capacity

About and limits

Gap = burden percentile minus capacity percentile; positive means burden outpaces capacity. Burden combines CDC PLACES prevalence of heart disease, stroke, high blood pressure, diabetes, smoking and obesity. Capacity combines PLACES preventive-care use and insurance with HRSA primary-care shortage (HPSA) scores (county only). SVI is shown for context and is not part of the gap.

Not shown: Kentucky and Pennsylvania (no PLACES 2025 core estimates) and Connecticut (planning-region boundaries not yet loaded). CDC WONDER mortality is not yet included.

PLACES values are model-based estimates. The gap is a prioritization index, not a causal claim, and the top-decile set depends on the HPSA weight (see the "without HPSA" view and the methods doc).
